The third in a series of ambient pieces made with a TASCAM 388 reel-to-reel. The process is simple: record a drone or melodic fragment, let the tape saturate, loop it, and layer. The machine does most of the work.

Vol. 3 is slower and quieter than the previous two. It was made over a few evenings in January, mostly as a way of not thinking about anything else.
