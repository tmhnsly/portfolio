Dreamscene is a story-development app for the messy front end of writing. You scatter ideas as cards, find their shape with Dan Harmon's Story Circle, read them back as a reel, then draft scenes in focused sprints. The idea running through it is chaos to order.

The Desk has a rich-text editor for prose and a screenplay editor that formats as you type: Enter moves from scene heading to action to character to dialogue, Tab cycles the element, and a script exports to Fountain for Final Draft. Cards drag and reorder, and the whole thing runs in the browser with no sign-up, saving locally, or to Supabase when it's configured, behind one data interface.

I designed it as well as built it. I wanted it to feel more like a quiet editing room than a productivity tool, so it uses an editorial serif, a dark palette and very little chrome.
