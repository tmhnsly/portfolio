A portfolio site for a London-based ceramicist. The brief was simple: let the work be the loudest thing on the page. Everything else — layout, typography, navigation — steps back.

Content is managed through a custom Sanity studio, built so the client can update work and press coverage without any help. Next.js handles the rendering; Tailwind keeps the stylesheet small and honest.
