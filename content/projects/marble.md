A small interactive 3D experiment. A marble sits at the top of a procedurally generated hill and rolls according to the physics of where you point it. The terrain is generated fresh on each load using fractional Brownian motion.

Built as an excuse to get deeper into React Three Fiber and write custom GLSL shaders. The lighting is entirely procedural — no baked textures, just math and time.
