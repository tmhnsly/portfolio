Fifteen frames from a long weekend in Lisbon. Shot on Portra 400 with the Leica M6. The light there in February is low and soft in a way that makes everything look like it was staged.

The set is deliberately small — fifteen frames from three rolls, edited down to what felt true rather than comprehensive.
