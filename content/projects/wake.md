Wake is a 14-minute documentary about wakeboarding, made at university with my housemate Max Wilman. He shot and cut the film. I wrote most of the music.

Five tracks, written, recorded, mixed and mastered at home in Logic Pro X with guitar and bass. A mix of instrumental post-rock, indie rock and ambient. One track, from 9:30 to 11:03, isn't mine.
