Wake is a 14-minute documentary about wakeboarding, made at university with my housemate Max Wilman. He shot and cut the film; I scored all of it.

## Scored start to finish

The music runs the full length of the film — instrumental post-rock, indie rock and ambient, written to move with the edit. I wrote, recorded, mixed and mastered it at home in Logic Pro X, with guitar and bass. The one stretch that isn't mine is the track from 9:30 to 11:03.

Scoring all fourteen minutes let the music hold the film's mood and pace throughout, instead of dropping in for the odd cue.
