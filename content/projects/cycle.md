A tiny puzzle game about cycling through states. You move a token around a grid; each cell changes the token's form in a different way. The goal is to reach the exit in a specific state. Made over a long bank holiday weekend in Godot.

It's short — maybe twenty minutes — and deliberately unpolished. The point was to see how far Godot's 2D tooling could take me in 48 hours. The answer: pretty far.
