Inspired by South Park's "6 Days to Air", we set ourselves a challenge: make a whole animated stand-up entirely on iPad, and see how workable the workflow really was. Scripted with ChatGPT, voiced with ElevenLabs, animated in ToonSquid, cut in LumaFusion, with sound effects in GarageBand. No original score, just the effects.

Made with my friend Nathan Heape as Hyper Chums, our animation channel. Nathan led the art; I handled the cutting and animation, and we co-directed and figured out the pipeline together.
