Less about the content, more a test: could we make a whole animated stand-up entirely on iPads, and how feasible was the workflow? Scripted with ChatGPT, voiced with ElevenLabs, animated in ToonSquid and scored in GarageBand, all on iPad.

Made with my friend Nathan Heape as Hyper Chums, our animation channel. Nathan led the art; I handled the cutting and animation, and we co-directed and figured out the pipeline together.
