Inspired by South Park's "6 Days to Air", we set ourselves a challenge: make a whole animated stand-up entirely on iPad, and see how feasible the workflow was. Scripted with ChatGPT, voiced with ElevenLabs, animated in ToonSquid, cut together in LumaFusion. No score, but the sound effects were built and exported in GarageBand.

Made with my friend Nathan Heape as Hyper Chums, our animation channel. Nathan led the art; I handled the cutting and animation, and we co-directed and figured out the pipeline together.
