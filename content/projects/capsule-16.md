A year-long learning exercise with one rule: release a film every month. After a Sound Design degree and a house full of filmmakers, this was where the filmmaking started.

Each month closed by picking one thing to improve next: composition, editing, camera movement. January to June were shot on an iPhone 6S with Filmic Pro; July to December moved to a Lumix G7. Twelve films, twelve months.

The full series is on [the YouTube playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL1UtQjgCwfLsIGWC5BgFNvTeCX1gTqMQN).
