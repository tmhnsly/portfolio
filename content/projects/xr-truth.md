My then housemate turned old clothes and bed sheets into banners, stamped with custom Extinction Rebellion designs we 3D printed, then hung them around New Malden to raise awareness ahead of the 2019 protests. I support the cause but didn't fancy getting arrested, so I volunteered my video skills and made this.
