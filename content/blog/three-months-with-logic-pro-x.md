On switching from Ableton, the things that took me by surprise, and the macOS shortcuts I've quietly learned to love.

I made the switch reluctantly, mostly because a friend pointed out that I was fighting Ableton's MIDI region editing every session. Three months later I don't miss Ableton for composition work, though I still reach for it when I want to do something with clips and scenes.

The thing Logic gets most right is the score editor, which I almost never use but appreciate existing. The thing it gets most wrong is the channel strip, which feels designed by committee. The shortcuts are mostly muscle memory now.

Would I go back? Probably not for the things I'm doing right now. Ask me again in another three months.
